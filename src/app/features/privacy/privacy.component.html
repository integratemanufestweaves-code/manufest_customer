How we use your data

Your account
Your mobile number and name, to sign you in and to contact you about your orders.
Your orders
Delivery addresses and order history. The seller and the courier see only what they need to deliver your order.
Payments
Online payments are handled by Razorpay. We don't store your card or UPI details.
Your city
Read from your default address, so we can show you products from weavers across other districts first.
Site statistics
Only if you allow analytics cookies: the pages you view, your device type and how you reached us (for example an Instagram link). These use random ids, never your name or phone number.

To see, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw a consent, email manufest@gmail.com.
